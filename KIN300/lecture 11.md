[[KIN300]]


lecture on *resistance exercise prescription* (workout plan)

simple variables for an exercise plan
- frequency (2-3 days - 4-7 days) distribution (what muscles are you focusing on)
- exercise selection
- exercise order
- training load and repetitions
- volume
- rest

*frequency:*
- beginner: 2-3 sessions/week
- intermediate: 3-4 sessions/week
- advanced: 4-7 sessions/week
more like rules of thumbs

*exercise distribution*
- how exercises selected for a program are distributed across the week
- impacted by the choice of *split used*
	- upper-lower: limits distribution
	- full body: the most freedom
		- skewed full-body: all muscle groups but preferring upper or lower 

- ideally training each muscle group > twice a week to improve muscle fitness
- volume is more important than frequency

*exercise selection:*
- should just pick based on what the individual knows 
types of categories:
- primary: most imporatnt
- secondary: required for performance improvement
- auxiliary: may support some aspects of the primary, rehab or injury prevention
- torso: for stabilization
general movements:
- lower body push/pull
- upper body push/pull both horizontal and vertical
- torso, abs really

*exercise order:*
- do heavier/faster compound 
- secondary compound movements are performed next
- isolation movements are performed last
- auxiliary movements used as rest

other:
- multi-joint then single-joint or compound then isolation movements
- higher skills/technique, concentration, effort first
- large then small muscle areas

order of exercise prescribed
- go from hardest to easiest
- consider fatigue
use supersets

for more experienced lifters you can add more load and decrease the reps

*intensity:* is measured as percentage of one rep max but also can just use rep ranges with perceived rate of exertion

*volume:* is expressed as the total amount of some loading variable per unit time
- ex: sets per week per muscle/muscle group 
volume-load: is the total number of sets multiplied by the number of reps per set then multiplied by the load 

*rest periods:* depend on the training goal
- strength: 2-5 min
- power (single or multiple effort): 2-5 min
- hypertrophy: 30s to 1.5 min
- muscular endurance: <= 30s

so in reality the things to prescribe are:
**frequency:** how many times are you training
**split:** what is the distribution of muscles you are training
**exercises:** primary, secondary, auxiliary, torso
**exercise order:** really just do hard -> easy
**reps and intensity:** based on the skill level of the person
**rest times:** based on the goal of the person
