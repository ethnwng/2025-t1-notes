### Cardio system
Mainly *supports* the endurance or aerobic system which is for long term exercise or energy output

cardio - heart
vascular - blood vessels

cardiovascular - how the blood is pumped throughout the body
- it transports and delivers stuff (oxygen, glucose, 
- protection
- homeostasis (temperature regulation)

heart pumps about 5L / min of blood, in strenuous exercise this increases to about 3x

components of the system:
- blood vessels (road)
- heart (pumper)
- blood (cars)

simple cycle:
- blood is oxygenated in the lungs
- oxygenated blood is sent to the heart
- heart pumps it out to the rest of the body
- deoxygenated blood returns to the heart which then goes back to the lungs and then does it again

*pulmonary arteries:* vessels that connect blood in lungs to the heart
*aorta:* a vessel that carries oxygenated blood throughout the body

more on blood:
- mainly composed of water >90%
- plasma proteins, and other stuff
- mainly focusing on erythrocytes *red blood cells (hemoglobin)*

*hemoglobin:* has the function transporting gas (oxygen)

*arteries:* always carry *oxygenated blood*
*veins:* carry *deoxygenated blood*

arteries sort of shrink into *capiliaries*

how heart work:
- right side of the heart receives deoxygenated blood
- sequence:
	- vena cava (inferior and superior, a large vein that enters the right side of the heart) (deoxygenated)
	- right atrium and ventricle (deoxygenated)
	- pulmonary artery (deoxygenated)
	- pulmonary veins (oxygenated)
	- left atrium and ventricle (oxygenated)
	- aorta (oxygenated)
- blood enters the right atrium into the right ventricle and out the pulmonary artery to the lungs

left side is much larger than right side of the heart even though the same volume of blood is pumped out.

##### Cardiac Output
The total blood flow is equal to the cardiac output.

the volume outputted by the heart is about 5L/min
- increases to like 25L/min during strenuous exercise (thought it was 3x but idk)

the cardiac output can be outputted as
$$
CO = HR \times SV
$$
- HR is heart rate and SV is stroke volume, the volume of blood per beat

trained athletes have better cardiac output.

when we exercise the amount of blood distributed to the body changes.
- during exercise *more blood is sent to the muscles*

the way the heart contracts is like a *twisting* move


*autonomic nervous system*
- parasympathetic nervous system: controls the heart when we are relaxed 
- sympathetic nervous system: controls the heart during fight or flight scenarios

the back of the head on the brainstem needs to be protected since they connect directly to the heart
- vagus nerve part of the parasympathetic nervous system
