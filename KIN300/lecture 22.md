**use**

- filtering debris
- a "resevoir" for blood
- maintaining pH and acid-balance 
- speaking/vocalization (need lungs to make noise)

**breathing mechanics**
- inspiration: taking a breath in, ur diaphram goes up and makes the lungs suck in air. an active process which we need to think about to do
- expiration: we relax our diaphram and push all the air out, passive or resting process
if we are exercising we do the expiration phase with more force and it becomes an active process that we have to think abt

*tidal volume:* the amount of air that moves in and out of the lungs at rest

*minute ventilation:* is the volume of air breathed each minute which can be increased by the respiratory rate (RR) and tidal volume ($V_{t}$) the formula for minute ventilation $V_{e}$ is
$$
V_{e} = {RR} \times V_{t}
$$
- respiratory rate increases to 35-40 $V_{e}$ during strenuous exercise, (60-70 for trained athletes)
- tidal rate rarely exceeds 60% for a trained or untrained
- minute ventilation reaches 110-120 for untrained and >150 for trained
