more on interpreting *odds*

- odds as a ratio is pretty much $\Large\frac{\text{number of success}}{\text{number of failure}}$
if the odds of something occuring is $0.25=\frac{1}{4}$ which is equivalent to a 1 in 5 $(4+1)$ chance of surviving. (sum the ratio to get the total)
